Returns & Refunds

Simple guidance for return, replacement and refund requests.

Before requesting a return

Keep your order ID or invoice details and contact FLASH GEAR BD as soon as possible if an item arrives damaged, incorrect or has a covered functional issue.

Eligibility

Return or replacement eligibility depends on the product condition, warranty terms and the reason for the request. Physical or liquid damage, misuse and unauthorized modification may affect eligibility.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, the refund method and timing will be explained by FLASH GEAR BD after the request is reviewed.

How to request help

Contact us through WhatsApp with your order ID, a short description of the issue and photos or videos when useful.
