Full Warranty Policy

Clear warranty information for products sold by FLASH GEAR BD.

Important: The warranty period for each product is the period shown on its product listing or sales invoice. Warranty terms may vary by product or brand, so please check the product information before ordering.
1. Warranty Period

Warranty coverage is provided for the period stated on the product listing or invoice.

2. What the Warranty Covers

Warranty covers eligible manufacturing or functional defects that occur during normal and intended use of the product.

3. What the Warranty Does Not Cover

Warranty does not cover physical damage, cracks, liquid or moisture damage, burn or electrical damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, improper installation, unauthorized repair or modification, tampering, or normal wear and tear.

4. Warranty Claim Requirements

To request warranty service, provide the original invoice, order details or other valid proof of purchase and the product. We may also ask for photos or videos to understand the issue before inspection.

5. Warranty Inspection

Every claim may be inspected to confirm whether the reported issue is covered by the warranty. A claim can be accepted only when the issue meets the applicable warranty conditions.

6. Repair or Replacement

If a covered defect is confirmed, FLASH GEAR BD will arrange the applicable warranty solution, which may be repair, replacement or another suitable resolution depending on the product, brand and availability.

7. Product Condition During Claim

The product should be provided in a condition that allows inspection. Accessories or components may be required when they are relevant to diagnosing the warranty issue.

8. Data & Personal Information

Customers should back up important data before handing over a device for inspection or service. FLASH GEAR BD is not responsible for loss of personal data during servicing.

9. Warranty Exclusions

Warranty is not a guarantee against accidental damage, misuse or ordinary deterioration. Specific brand or product warranty conditions may apply in addition to this general policy.

10. How to Start a Claim

Contact FLASH GEAR BD through WhatsApp and send your order/invoice details together with a short description of the problem. Our team will guide you through the next step.

11. Final Warranty Decision

Warranty eligibility is determined after reviewing the product condition and the applicable warranty terms. If a claim is outside warranty coverage, we will explain the reason when possible.

Note: Warranty terms can differ by product and brand. The product listing or invoice takes priority for the specific warranty period and any product-specific conditions.
